WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

The research material this site was built from contained no photography at all —
no logo, no food photos, no interior shots. Every photograph currently on the
site is an openly-licensed stand-in from Wikimedia Commons showing the *type* of
dish, not JeunJu's own food. They are credited at /credits.

They are good enough to launch behind, and wrong to keep. Replace them with the
shots below and the site becomes genuinely the restaurant's own.

HOW TO REPLACE A PHOTO

  1. Name the file after the id in the "id" column below, e.g. gamjatang.jpg
  2. Drop it in .media-cache/replacements/
  3. Run: node scripts/harvest-media.mjs
     (local replacements are picked up ahead of anything downloaded)
  4. Delete the corresponding entry from /credits once nothing sourced remains.

SHOOTING NOTES

  · Overhead (flat-lay) for anything in a bowl. These are soups and stews — a
    three-quarter angle hides the contents, which is the whole point of the dish.
  · Natural light, no flash, no coloured gels. The site sits on a true obsidian
    ground with a champagne-gold accent, so warm-toned photographs glow on it and
    anything shot cool or grey goes flat.
  · Dark, uncluttered backgrounds photograph best here — the design lets food sit
    in a pool of light, and a bright busy background fights that.
  · Shoot on the restaurant's real tableware and real tables. Do not style with
    props the restaurant does not own.
  · Landscape or square. Portrait crops get cropped anyway.
  · Minimum 1600px on the long edge. Straight out of a recent phone is fine.
  · Include the banchan wherever it comes with the dish. It is the selling point.
